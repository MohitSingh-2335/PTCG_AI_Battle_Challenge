# Strategy Track Summary

The strategy competition is the writeup/report track. It evaluates how clearly you explain the agent, the deck, and the reasoning behind the design.

Key points:
- Participation in the simulation competition is required to enter the strategy competition.
- The submission is a Kaggle writeup, not a code upload.
- The writeup should include a title, subtitle, and detailed analysis.
- The writeup must be 2000 words or less.
- A media gallery is optional.

Evaluation emphasis:
- Model score: clarity of the chosen approach, technical soundness, stability, and competition performance.
- Deck score: how well the deck concept supports the intended strategy.
- Report score: clarity, structure, and use of figures or tables.

Practical implication:
- A strong strategy submission should explain why the deck works, what assumptions it depends on, what was tested, and why the chosen heuristic or training approach is credible.
